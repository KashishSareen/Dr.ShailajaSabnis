Consultation & specialized management guided by Dr. Shailaja Sabnis, an eminent Rheumatologist with over 30 years of clinical experience.

What is Rheumatology?

There are over 200 types of Auto Immune conditions, including Rheumatoid Arthritis, Gout, Lupus, Ankylosing Spondylitis, Osteoporosis, Tendinitis, Vasculitis, and inherited connective tissue disorders.

Medical Specialization

What are Autoimmune Diseases?

Your immune system is built to protect you from infections like viruses and bacteria. In autoimmune conditions, the immune system mistakenly identifies healthy cells as foreign invaders.

This misfire causes inflammation and potential damage to organs, joints, tissues, or blood vessels. Modern rheumatology focuses on managing medical immunology to restore internal balance.

Immunological Health

What Causes These Conditions?

While precise causes vary, autoimmune conditions frequently have a genetic baseline combined with environmental factors.

  • Genetic Predisposition: Tendency to run in family lineages.
  • Environmental Triggers: Exposure to viral infections or specific chemicals.
