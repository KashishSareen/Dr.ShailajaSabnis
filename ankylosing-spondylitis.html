03 / Spondyloarthritis

Ankylosing Spondylitis

Ankylosing spondylitis can cause inflammatory back pain, stiffness and reduced movement. Early assessment helps guide treatment and protect function.

Discuss your symptoms
Consultation focusSpinal inflammation, mobility and individualized therapy.

What we look for

Back pain has different patterns.

Morning stiffness, pain that improves with movement and symptoms beginning at a younger age can suggest inflammatory back pain. Assessment may include examination, blood tests and imaging.

Care approach

Keep movement part of the plan.

Treatment combines appropriate medication, activity guidance and monitoring of symptoms and function. The plan evolves with your response and goals.

Next step

Understand the source of persistent back stiffness.

A consultation can help distinguish inflammatory symptoms from other causes of back pain.
