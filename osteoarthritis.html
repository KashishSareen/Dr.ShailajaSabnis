04 / Joint health

Osteoarthritis

Osteoarthritis is a common joint condition involving pain, stiffness and changes in joint function. A tailored plan can help preserve movement and independence.

Discuss your symptoms
Consultation focusPain relief, mobility and practical joint care.

What we look for

Understand the source of joint pain.

Pain with activity, stiffness and reduced range of movement can affect daily life. Examination and a careful history help identify the most useful next step.

Care approach

Build a plan around your movement.

Management may include medical treatment, activity changes, weight and strength guidance, and referrals when another specialist opinion is useful.

Next step

Make movement easier to return to.

Bring your concerns, previous reports and medication list to a consultation.
