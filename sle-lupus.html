02 / Autoimmune disease

SLE (Lupus)

Systemic lupus erythematosus is an autoimmune condition that can affect different parts of the body. Care focuses on understanding the pattern of symptoms and protecting organ health.

Discuss your symptoms
Consultation focusFlare management, organ review and long-term prevention.

What we look for

Symptoms can change over time.

Fatigue, joint pain, rashes, fever and other symptoms may have several causes. A structured history, examination and appropriate investigations help build a clear picture.

Care approach

Steady monitoring makes care safer.

Treatment is tailored to disease activity and the organs involved. Medication review and regular monitoring help manage flares while supporting everyday wellbeing.
