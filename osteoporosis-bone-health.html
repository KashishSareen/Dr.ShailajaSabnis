05 / Bone health

Osteoporosis & Bone Health

Bone health care combines risk assessment, appropriate testing and treatment designed to reduce fracture risk and support strength through every stage of life.

Consultation focusBone density, fracture risk and prevention.

What we look for

Your fracture risk is individual.

Age, family history, previous fractures, medicines, nutrition and other health conditions all inform a bone health assessment.

Care approach

Protect bone health with a complete plan.

Care may include investigations, medication where appropriate, nutrition and safe strength or balance activity guidance.
