06 / Crystal arthritis

Gout & Hyperuricemia

Gout is a form of inflammatory arthritis linked with urate crystal deposits. Prompt flare care and long-term management can reduce recurrence and joint damage.

Discuss your symptoms
Consultation focusFlare relief and sustainable uric acid control.

What we look for

Sudden pain has a pattern.

Rapid swelling, warmth and severe pain in a joint can be signs of gout, but other conditions can look similar. Assessment helps guide the right response.

Care approach

Control today and prevent tomorrow.

Care may include flare treatment, urate-lowering therapy when appropriate, monitoring and a discussion of factors that affect recurrence.

Next step

Do not let recurring flares become your normal.

A consultation can clarify the cause of painful joint episodes and the next step.
