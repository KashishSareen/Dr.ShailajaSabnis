07 / Skin–joint disease

Psoriatic Arthritis

Psoriatic arthritis links joint inflammation with psoriasis. Identifying the pattern early helps protect joints and improve comfort.

Discuss your symptoms All services

Consultation focus

Joint and skin pattern recognition with coordinated care.

What we look for

Joint and skin symptoms may connect.

Swollen joints, morning stiffness, enthesitis, nail changes or psoriasis history can point toward psoriatic arthritis rather than isolated osteoarthritis.

Care approach

Coordinate care around your whole body.

Treatment is chosen for joint activity and skin involvement, often with dermatology collaboration when needed.

In clinic, we also review

A clearer path from symptoms to plan.

  • Enthesitis and dactylitis review
  • Nail and skin clues
  • Joint-protection treatment planning
  • Dermatology-aware follow-up

Next step

Bring the whole pattern into focus.

A consultation can connect skin and joint symptoms into one clear plan.
