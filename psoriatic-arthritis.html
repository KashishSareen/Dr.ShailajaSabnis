07 / Inflammatory arthritis

Psoriatic Arthritis

Psoriatic arthritis can affect joints, tendons, the spine and skin. Coordinated care helps address the full pattern rather than one symptom at a time.

Discuss your symptoms
Consultation focusJoint symptoms, skin disease and function.

What we look for

Joint and skin symptoms may connect.

Swelling, stiffness, nail changes, psoriasis and tendon pain can all be relevant. A detailed assessment helps identify the pattern of inflammation.

Care approach

Coordinate care around your whole body.

Treatment is planned around affected areas, disease activity and your daily needs, with dermatology collaboration when useful.

Next step

Bring the whole pattern into focus.
